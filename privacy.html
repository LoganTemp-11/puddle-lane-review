Privacy policy

Puddle Lane, 109 High Street, Falkirk. Last updated 14 September 2026.

The short version. This website does not collect anything about you by itself. It has no forms, no accounts and no cookies. If you email or phone the shop, we see what you send us and nothing more. The map on the front page comes from a mapping company and only loads if you ask for it.

Who we are

Puddle Lane is an independent gift shop at 109 High Street, Falkirk, FK1 1ED, run by Liza. For anything to do with this policy, email puddlelane@yahoo.com or phone 01324 227300.

What this site collects

Nothing directly. There is no contact form, no newsletter box, no login and no online shop. The buttons that say "Ask Liza to put it by" open your own email app with a message already written for you. Nothing is sent until you press send in that app, and it goes straight to the shop's email address.

When you email or phone us

We will see your name, your email address or phone number, and whatever you tell us. We use that to answer you, for example to confirm we have put something aside for you. We keep those messages in the shop's email account for as long as we need them to deal with your request, then delete them. We do not add you to any mailing list and we do not pass your details to anyone else.

Cookies and the map

This site sets no cookies. One thing is saved in your own browser: your answer to the small "cookie choice" box at the bottom of the page, so it does not ask you again. That is stored on your device, not sent to us, and you can clear it through your browser at any time or change it with the "Cookie choice" link at the foot of the page.

The map on the front page is drawn with map tiles from CARTO, using OpenStreetMap data. The tiles load only after you say yes in the cookie choice box or press "Show the map". When they load, your browser requests them from CARTO's servers, so CARTO sees your IP address and basic browser details the way any website you visit does. CARTO's own policy is at carto.com/privacy. If you would rather not, use the "Get directions" or "Open larger map" links instead, which take you to Google Maps on your own terms.

Visitor counting

None at the moment. If we ever add a visitor counter it will be a privacy-friendly one that does not set cookies or follow you across other sites, it will only switch on if you have said yes in the cookie choice box, and this page will be updated to say so.

Hosting

The site is served by Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare keeps short-lived technical logs (such as IP addresses and the pages requested) to keep the service running and secure. Cloudflare's policy is at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

Links to other sites

The Instagram, Facebook and Google Maps links take you to those companies' sites, which have their own privacy policies. We do not control what they collect.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email puddlelane@yahoo.com and we will sort it. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If anything on this page changes we will update the date at the top.
